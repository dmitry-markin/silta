# Contributing

- Open an issue first for anything beyond a one-line fix, so the design is agreed before the code exists. Questions are welcome in [Discussions](https://github.com/dmitry-markin/silta/discussions/categories/q-a). Unannounced PRs may be closed unread.
- Contributions written with AI are welcome when the author understands and has tested every line and says so in the PR. PRs the author can't explain will be closed.
- Submit small PRs, one change each: ideally under 300 lines and never above 1000. Keep the description concise and in your own words. The title is the changelog line:
  - a prefix naming the directory or component (`siltad:`, `silta-contract-check:`, `docs:`), then a capitalised imperative without a full stop, 70 characters at most, prefix included;
  - exactly one of the labels `added`, `changed`, `fixed`, `removed`;
  - changes too small for the changelog take the prefix `minor:` with a lower-case first word (`minor: fix a typo in README`) and the label `minor`.
- Contributions are accepted under the project's `Apache-2.0 OR MIT` licence, as stated in the README.
